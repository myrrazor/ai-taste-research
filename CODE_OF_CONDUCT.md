# Code Of Conduct

NanoTaste discussions should stay practical, respectful, and focused on the
work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Keep critique tied to code, docs, tests, or experiment design.
- Respect privacy boundaries around taste files, prompts, candidate text, and
  calibration notes.
- Assume mistakes are fixable and document them clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private prompts, taste files, or security reports without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Using the project to distribute malware, credentials, or private data.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may remove comments, close issues, block users, or decline
contributions that violate this code. Security or privacy violations may be
handled privately first to reduce harm.
